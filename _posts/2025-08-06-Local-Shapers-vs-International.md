This will be my take on the local shaper vs. national/international brands debate. There's been a considerable push for, particularly beginners, to start going to local shapers instead of purchasing off the rack at their local shop. In some ways, I agree with this. My local shapers, from an easy to get to custom only, range from Rozbern and WRV to Brian Wynn and In The Eye surfboards. There's certainly more than that, many of them good. I don't want this to get into a "this shaper is good, that one bad" thing.

There are benefits to going to a local shaper. They, in theory, should have boards that are better suited to local waves. Mainly, that means that at least the rocker will work. Whether or not you pick the board that matches the types of waves you actually intend to surf is still up in the air. Another benefit, is that off the rack local shapes tend to have stronger glassing (at least non-performance shapes). If the board at least *works*, then it lasting longer is a great thing. It, of course, leads to better resale value as well.

That said, of the several locally made boards I own, I have only kept one of them long term. My favorite groveler is made by Lost and produced in Vietnam. My favorite daily driver style board is from Doc at Surf Prescriptions. I will say that in terms of barrel boards, the Rozbern Hound has been good. I like that one. At the end of the day, my primary concern for my boards is how well they work. I like supporting local business if I can. I work for a small business and I get how important they are for the local economy. But at the end of the day, the board has to go well. We don't get a lot of swell on the East Coast, and when we do, I don't want to be out there with a board I don't like or worse, one I don't trust to get me in on big days. I don't mind if a board is made overseas. I'd rather it be made in America if I had my way, but at then end of the day, quality is the most imporatant variable for me. I have a Roberts board I had to patch before I even took it out for the first time because it wasn't water tight upon purchase (I knew about it, but it was on sale). If national brands can't do the QC required to put out a good product and the international ones do, where should your money go?
