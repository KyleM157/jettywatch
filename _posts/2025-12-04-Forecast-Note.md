## A Look at the Current and Future Jet Streams

![20251208 Jet Stream](/assets/images/20251208_jetstream_resized.jpg)

Courtesy of tropicaltidbits.com

While I don't have as nice of a historical jet stream image as I'd like, this is an ok example of what we've had for the past month or so (though broken up a bit). The jet stream is a band of high speed wind that helps to create and move storms from west-east across the U.S. As you can see, there's a strong band of wind aimed north, directly over New Jersey. If you are a low pressure system over the great plains, you're going to follow that track right out of our swell window.

However, look here:

![20251212 Jet Stream](/assets/images/20251214_resized.jpg)

Courtesy of tropicaltidbits.com

That there is the GEFS ensemble forecast for 12/12, showing a well put-together jetstream south of NJ, roughly over the border of VA and NC. This is an ideal storm track for our region. I'm seeing a few instances of that pattern over the GFS 16 day forecast window. Hopefully this is the start of a pattern that puts more swell producing systems in our swell window rather than north of it.

## What's to Come

I'm seeing a decent set up on the charts for 12/10 and another (mediocre) setup for 12/15. Whether these set ups translate to swell producing systems in our area is another question.

The main takeaway is we're starting to see evidence that more swell producing systems are on the way and the pattern of low pressure systems staying too far to the north could finally be shifting. There's strong evidence that the La Nina pattern is coming to a close and we should be in an ENSO neutral pattern soon and certainly by Jan/Feb. The east coast doesn't have quite the ENSO-Swell correlation that the west coast does, but any change in whatever pattern we've had is a welcome change as far as I'm concerned.

We also have warmer sea surface temperatures off NC which could help fuel storm activity. Though we also have colder SSTs off our coast, so keep some thick neoprene nearby.
